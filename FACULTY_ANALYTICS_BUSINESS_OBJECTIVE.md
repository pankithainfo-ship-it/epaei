# Faculty Analytics Application

## Business Objective
The Faculty Analytics application is designed to help academic and training administrators manage faculty workload, schedule planning, and teaching capacity with greater visibility and control. The system brings together faculty batch assignments and faculty availability data into a single dashboard so administrators can quickly identify active instructors, workload distribution, course coverage, and free-time patterns.

The core business goal is to improve planning efficiency, reduce scheduling errors, and ensure that faculty resources are assigned optimally across batches, courses, and time slots.

## Why This Application Exists
Educational institutions and training programs often face challenges such as:
- faculty schedules being spread across multiple batches and courses
- no clear picture of who is currently teaching, upcoming, or already completed
- difficulty identifying underutilized or overloaded faculty members
- limited visibility into available teaching hours for future planning
- manual effort required to analyze course and weekday distribution

This application addresses these issues by turning operational schedule data into visual analytics that support faster decisions.

## User Problems
### 1. Unclear faculty workload distribution
Administrators often struggle to understand how many batches each faculty member is handling and whether workload is balanced across the team.

### 2. Poor scheduling visibility
Without a structured view of current, upcoming, and completed batches, it is difficult to know which faculty are fully assigned, free, or at risk of overload.

### 3. Course coverage gaps
When multiple batches run simultaneously, managers may not immediately know which courses are growing, underrepresented, or concentrated in a few faculty members.

### 4. Limited availability planning
Faculty free-time and availability data is usually managed in disconnected formats. This makes it hard to plan new batches, evaluate teaching capacity, and optimize time allocation.

### 5. Time-consuming manual reporting
Without interactive analytics, teams spend valuable time manually collecting and comparing schedule information from multiple sources.

## Proposed Solution
The application provides a centralized dashboard with the following capabilities:

- faculty filter selection for viewing a single faculty or the full team
- summary metrics such as total batches, represented faculty, and available hours
- batch status breakout by Current, Upcoming, and Completed
- course distribution chart to reveal which subjects are most active
- free-time analysis by weekday to show when faculty are available
- live data retrieval from academic scheduling APIs
- a simple visual structure that allows administrators to interpret trends quickly

## Business Benefits
- Better workforce planning for academic operations
- Faster decision-making on faculty allocation
- Improved utilization of teaching resources
- Reduced risk of overbooking or underutilization
- More transparent academic scheduling across batches and departments
- Greater confidence in planning future programs, sessions, and faculty assignments

## Summary
This application solves the operational challenge of managing faculty assignments and availability in a data-driven way. By combining batch status, teaching load, course distribution, and free-time insights into a unified analytics dashboard, it helps institutions plan more effectively and allocate faculty resources with confidence.
